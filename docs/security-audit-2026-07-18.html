LOW SEC-0718-2 Rate-limiter IP keyed on the un-spoofable proxy-appended hop (CWE-348)
Finding: clientIp() keyed the per-IP fixed-window limiter on the LEFTMOST x-forwarded-for hop. Fix: clientIp() now takes the RIGHTMOST XFF hop (the address Caddy appends), which cannot be spoofed behind one appending proxy.
