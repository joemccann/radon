Evidence to decision

Radon’s research workbench carries source passages through analysis, scenarios, exported artifacts and operator trading review. Select a stage to inspect the implemented workflows.

Keep the source intact

  • Import a filing, transcript, plaintext email, desk note or existing Radon newsfeed summary. Save and restore validated evidence JSON.
  • Research PDFs produce hashed manifests of original-page passages and tables. Authenticated retrieval excludes pages that require OCR.
  • Private-company cards retain the financing subject, amount, stage and investors with exact quoted evidence.

Premium subscriptions and gated documents require existing access. Source content is data, never an instruction to an agent.

Trace each figure

  • Cited briefs link each claim to its literal source passage and document date.
  • Fundamentals bind metrics, signed values, currency scales and fiscal periods. EBITDA reconciliations expose missing components and exclusions.
  • Earnings and buyback blackout dates become calendar holds only when explicitly stated; export them as ICS.
  • Transcript themes are review candidates. The configured assistant receives a bounded, date-filtered research packet for adversarial reasoning.
  • AI-spending comparisons join capex and revenue within a common period. General company capex is not labeled AI-only.

Turn reviewed work into artifacts

  • Edit DCF and simplified LBO assumptions. Enter comparable companies and apply their median EV/EBITDA multiple.
  • Download an XLSX with editable assumptions, formulas, cached results, peer inputs and source passages.
  • Export Snapshot, What’s moving, Pipeline and Headwinds to XLSX or PPTX. Portfolio snapshots retain their timestamp and cannot leak into earlier analysis dates.
  • Labs drafts survive tab changes. Evidence JSON saves source documents and facts; model assumptions travel in the scenario workbook.

Keep the operator at the order boundary

  • Apply a reviewed, ticker-bound checklist to the existing ticket. Context expires after 24 hours; it never submits or invents order fields.
  • Existing authentication, coverage, quote, order-risk and confirmation controls remain authoritative. Hosted MCP remains read-only.
  • Inspect bounded governance audit exports. Import MCP golden-query reports to review expected-response agreement, latency and failures.
  • The marketing section connects evidence, indicators, expression and operator-controlled execution.

Offline evaluation measures fixture execution. It does not establish live provider reliability or financial-fact accuracy.
